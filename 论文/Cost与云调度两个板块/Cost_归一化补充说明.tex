Under the adopted common-release policy, $R=\sum_jr_j$ is fixed and positive within an instance. When the reference floors are inactive, the normalized cost and makespan satisfy
\[
\frac{C(s)}{C(s_g)}=\frac{R H_{\mathrm{max}}(s)}{R H_{\mathrm{max}}(s_g)}
=\frac{H_{\mathrm{max}}(s)}{H_{\mathrm{max}}(s_g)}.
\]
Consequently the four-component scalarization is equivalent to
\[
F(s)=(w_{\mathrm{ms}}+w_{\mathrm{cost}})\frac{H_{\mathrm{max}}(s)}{c_{\mathrm{ms}}}
+w_{\mathrm{en}}\frac{E(s)}{c_{\mathrm{en}}}+w_{\mathrm{lb}}\frac{\mathcal I(s)}{c_{\mathrm{lb}}}.
\]
Nominal equal weights therefore give effective weights $(0.50,0.25,0.25)$ for makespan, energy, and imbalance. Cost remains a reported policy-dependent outcome but is not an independent objective. Its local non-worsening test is redundant with the makespan test apart from numerical tolerances. Retaining the preceding nominal weights makes this change reproducible; it does not preserve the preceding effective preference.
